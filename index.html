About this exploration

An inward journey.

Inspired by MKBHD and Epic Spaceman’s I shrunk down into an M5 chip.

This is an original, illustrative 3D reconstruction. The internal floorplan, dimensions, colors, and transistor cutaway are explanatory, not a reproduction of Apple’s proprietary chip layout. Scale changes are cinematic.

Apple describes M5 as using third-generation 3-nanometer technology. “3 nm” names a manufacturing generation; it is not the literal size of every transistor feature.

Read Apple’s M5 introduction

Materials are representative: silicon in the fin, doped semiconductor in source/drain regions, conductive metals in the gate and wiring, and an insulating gate dielectric. Hafnium-based high-k dielectrics are a common technology example; this does not identify Apple’s exact gate stack.

The atomic view uses a diamond-cubic silicon model. Spheres mark atoms and rods mark bonds; they are not literal surfaces. A NIST silicon reference gives a lattice constant near 0.543 nm. The nearest-neighbour spacing is a√3/4 ≈ 0.235 nm. These are bulk silicon reference values, not measurements of this illustrated chip.

Electrons are described by probability clouds, not fixed orbits or a bead with a measurable drawn diameter.
